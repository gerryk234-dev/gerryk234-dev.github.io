ANALYSIS · INTELLIGENCE

Analysis

A practical physical starting point — with clarity about what a mineral test can and cannot tell you.

THE STARTING POINT

Listen to the information your body can offer

Hair Tissue Mineral Analysis (HTMA) measures certain minerals and elements in a hair sample. DELTA uses this information as one part of a wider wellbeing conversation, alongside your intake information, lifestyle and nutritional needs.

Your original laboratory results are presented with a clear educational summary so you can better understand the measured values and explore appropriate next steps.

Important: HTMA is not a stand-alone medical diagnostic test. Hair mineral patterns do not reliably reveal personality traits, spiritual states or levels of consciousness.

MINERAL INTELLIGENCE

Understanding, not overwhelm

01 / MEASURE

See the minerals measured in your sample, together with the lab’s reference information.

02 / EXPLAIN

Your plain-English summary

Accessible commentary and educational resources explain relevant terms and limitations.

03 / SUPPORT

Your proposed programme

Personalised nutrition and supplement guidance is prepared according to defined rules and safety checks.

PERSONALISED AND RESPONSIBLE

Safety is part of the experience

Before personalised supplement guidance is issued, DELTA considers relevant health history, medicines, pregnancy and other contraindications. Results or situations requiring professional judgement are referred for review.

We also offer an optional paid practitioner voice note if you want individual commentary on your results.

Analysis is the beginning, not the destination.

THE SIX DELTA PILLARS

The Method

A grounded, practical approach to everyday wellbeing — taught through videos, guidance and simple repeatable practices.

ONE CONNECTED APPROACH

Six pillars. One evolving practice.

The DELTA Method is not about doing everything at once. It is an invitation to develop stronger everyday foundations, one practical step at a time. Each pillar offers short teaching videos, guided practices and material you can return to as you grow.

01 DELTA METHOD

BODY

Nourish & Strengthen

Give your body the nourishment, hydration and support it needs to build a stronger foundation.

Nutrition, hydration, appropriate supplement guidance, warm-to-cool showers and optional sauna education.

02 DELTA METHOD

ENVIRONMENT

Create Better Spaces

Make your home, surroundings and relationships places that support greater clarity and wellbeing.

Healthy indoor spaces, ventilation, damp and mould awareness, boundaries and supportive social environments.

03 DELTA METHOD

GROUND

Reconnect with Yourself and Nature

Step out of the noise, reconnect with nature and rediscover the power of being present.

Time outdoors, mindful attention, digital quiet, grounding practices and DELTA Energy Circuit visualisation.

04 DELTA METHOD

MOVE

Build Strength & Get the Energy Moving

Move in ways that build strength, free up energy and make you feel more alive in your body.

Walking, strength, mobility, posture, Qigong, stretching and enjoyable movement.

05 DELTA METHOD

RELEASE

Feel, Express & Let Go

Create space to notice emotions, soften tension, express yourself and welcome laughter back in.

Gentle emotional reflection, Body Tension Release, Stillness Technique and LAUGHOLOGY.

06 DELTA METHOD

REST

Restore & Renew

Give yourself permission to slow down so that rest, recovery and inner stillness become part of everyday life.

Sleep routines, breathing, relaxation, massage and restorative practices.

HOW IT FITS TOGETHER

Your personal starting point

Body, environment, nature, movement, emotional release and rest influence daily experience in different ways. DELTA brings practical learning together in one place, while recognising that people have different needs and circumstances.

Our guided practices are educational and optional. They do not replace appropriate medical or mental-health care.

WHY WE EXIST

A more conscious world begins with us

We live in an extraordinary age. Technology can connect us to knowledge and opportunity, yet constant distraction and life behind screens can also leave us feeling far apart — from nature, from each other and from ourselves.

At DELTA, we believe our future needs more than technological progress. It also needs compassion, presence, thoughtful choices and a deeper sense of human connection.

Our vision is to help grow a community of people who care for their physical foundations while remaining curious about consciousness, meaning and the bigger picture of human life.

THE DELTA VIEW

From physical support to inner exploration

Nutrition, movement, supportive environments and rest may help people create space for reflection and personal growth. Spiritual awakening, however, is an individual experience — not an outcome that a mineral test or programme can measure or guarantee.

You do not have to share any particular belief to belong here. DELTA welcomes thoughtful questions and differing perspectives.
